Why it matters

Blood

There are blood banks everywhere. Why bank mine?

Blood banks hold donor blood for about six weeks. We cryopreserve your own immune cells, plasma and DNA, in your name, for decades.

Why it could matter laterIf illness or demanding treatment depletes your immune system, your younger banked immune cells may help rebuild it.

Fibroblasts

What are fibroblasts, and why would I bank them?

They make your collagen, and they're the cells behind the Nobel Prize-winning discovery that adult cells can become stem cells again. They age with you, so the best time to bank them is now.

Why it could matter laterThe FDA has approved fibroblast therapies that produce new collagen. A future skin treatment could use your own cells.

Long reads and methylation

Why should I care how my DNA is read?

Consumer DNA tests read a tiny fraction of your genome. Long-read sequencing reads all of it, plus the methylation that shows how you're aging.

Why it could matter laterEvery future test can be compared against the day you banked, so you see how you're aging, not just where you stand.

The paired asset

Storage plus epigenetic analysis reveals how you age.

Your membership has always preserved your cells at the age you banked them. Now we can also read them: the Complete Baseline records your full genome and your methylation, the epigenetic marks that shift as you age. A testing company can read you today, but it can't go back and read the younger you. We can, because we're the custodian of your cells.

Other companies sell you a snapshot of your biology. We build a baseline that you can compare your future self to, forever.

What goes into your vault

Each draw is dated and stored as its own "vintage." Future draws add new vintages; they never replace the first.

2New · Fibroblast banking

A quick skin punch, grown into a lasting cell bank.

Skin cells drawn from a quick skin punch. Minimally invasive. Fibroblasts are the cells that build and maintain your skin, and they are among the most widely used starting cells for reprogramming research. Our lab expands early-passage fibroblasts and cryopreserves them alongside a piece of the original tissue.

Collection can be done by a registered nurse or dermatologist. No clinical setting or physician required.

Member questions

Common questions

Anything else? Reply to our email or write to hello@foreverlabs.com. It comes to our team directly.

Does anything change about my current membership?

No. Your existing plan, stored cells, and terms stay exactly as they are. Everything on this page is optional and adds to what you already have.

What is the Complete Baseline, and how is it different from a consumer DNA test?

It's a single deep 30× long-read run that reads your full genome and your methylation (epigenetic) marks together, from your own stored sample. Most consumer tests read a small subset of your DNA with short reads. Long reads resolve structural variation and hard-to-read regions. Because we keep both your sample and your data, future tests can run on them and be compared against this baseline.

The Complete Baseline is a member analysis service reviewed with a physician. It is not a diagnostic test.

Can you run the Complete Baseline on cells I banked years ago?

Yes, in most cases. We use one vial for the run and leave the rest untouched. We check DNA quality on that vial before charging the balance, so you don't pay the full price for a run we can't complete.

When will I receive my results?

Every Baseline runs on one instrument, one member at a time. When you reserve, we schedule your run and confirm your completion month. Your physician-reviewed report is delivered in that month.

How does the blood draw work?

A licensed phlebotomist comes to your home at a time you choose, or you visit a lab in your hometown. The draw takes about 15 minutes. Your sample ships to our lab, where it's processed into plasma, immune-cell, and DNA aliquots and cryopreserved.

If I add blood or fibroblasts, do I pay another storage fee?

No. If you store two or more collection types, you pay a single storage fee. Lifetime members who add blood are covered by their existing Lifetime storage.

I paid for stem-cell banking but never completed my procedure. What happens to that?

Your membership and stem-cell benefit stay in place. Blood banking is simply a faster way to get your biology into the vault now. Marrow collection remains available whenever you're ready.

Who owns and controls my stored material and data?

Your material and data are held in your name under your Forever Labs service agreement. We maintain custody of your genomic data in a highly secure environment so you can run future tests on it, and we will never share or sell your data without your consent.
